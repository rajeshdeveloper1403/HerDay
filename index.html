Happy Birthday, Nandhana!

Unakkaga enna ezhuthuradhu-nu romba neram yosichen...

It’s one special birthday for me too. ❤️

Adhanala, indha birthday-ku unakkaga konjam different-ah and special ah edhavadhu panna thonuchu. 🌻

So indha chinna surprise unakkaga... but adhu unakke therinjiduchu 😅🥰

Idhu periya vishayama irukkaama irukkalam.
Perfect-ah kuda irukkaama irukkalam.

Ana idha pannumbodhu, oru oru chinna detail-um yosichu dhaan pannen.

Yenna na...

Namakku pidichavangalukaga edukura chinna chinna efforts-kum oru thani meaning irukkum. ❤️✨

Unna pathi yosikumbodhu, unnaku theriyama unna patha moments and Unnoda spend panna time dhaan first-ah nyabagam varadhu.

Namma random conversations...
Namma silly talks... ☺️
Summa edhavadhu pesikitte irundha moments...
Reason-e illaama serndhu siricha moments... 😄
Sometimes pesama irundhaalum comfortable-ah feel panna moments... 🤍

Idhellam mathavangalukku normal-ah irukkalam.

Aana enakku mattum konjam special-ah feel aachi 😅❤️

Eppo idhellam nadandhuchu-nu enakkum theriyala...

Aana unkitta pesuradhu konjam konjam-ah oru habit aayiduchu.

Un message vandha udane, theriyama smile panradhu kooda oru habit aayiduchu. 😊

Nee illaama oru day irundha, adhu konjam different-ah feel aagudhu.

Idhellam naan solla vendiya vishayama-nu kooda theriyala...

Naan en feelings ellathayum open-ah solla therinja aal illa.

Sila feelings-ku words-e kidaiyadhu.
Sila vishayangala sollaama irukkaradhula kooda oru azhagu irukkum. 🌙

Adhanala...

Naan sollaama vittadhu konjamavadhu indha message moolama unakku puriya vaikkum-nu namburen. ❤️

Nee eppovume happy-ah irukkanum. 😊

Nee edhukkaga work pannitu irukkiyo, andha ellame unakku kidaikkanum. ✨

Neraya sirikkanum. 😁
Neraya beautiful memories create pannanum. 🌸

Andha memories-la...

Enakkum konjam place irundha, adha vida happy-ah irukka enakku vera edhuvum thevai illa. ❤️🥹

One day nee indha birthday-ah thirumbi paakumbodhu...

Indha little surprise unna oru vishayatha nyabagam paduthanum...

Yaaro oruthar unakkaga konjam extra time eduthu, oru oru chinna detail-um yosichu idhellam pannirukanga... 🌻❤️

Yenna na, nee avangalukku just another person illa. 🫶🏻❤️

Adhukku mela naan edhuvum sollala...

Sila vishayangalai explain pannaama purinjukaradhu dhaan romba azhaga irukkum. 😉🤍

Keep smiling. 😊

Always ipdiye crazy, cute, jolly and wonderful-ah iru. 🤗❤️

And...

En life-la oru beautiful memory-ah irundhadhukku thank you. 🤍✨

Innum neraya conversations...
Innum neraya silly moments...
Innum neraya laughs...

And hopefully, innum neraya memories — NAMMODADHU. ❤️🌻✨

— forever yours
